Why ProblemShared

This isn't
a career move.
It's a choice.

After a seven-figure exit, I don't need the next job. I'm choosing this one — because neurodevelopmental conditions have touched my family directly, and because I believe ProblemShared is building something that actually changes lives.

For fourteen years, I ran patient-acquisition campaigns for medical practices across the United States. The work wasn't abstract B2B marketing — it was healthcare consumers making high-stakes decisions in a regulated environment where clinical trust is the conversion barrier. That's exactly what ProblemShared faces as it launches D2C.

I understand the complexity of marketing compliantly in a clinical context without sacrificing commercial effectiveness. I've done it at scale. And I want to do it here, for a mission I believe in.

Regulated healthcare marketing
14 years operating within HIPAA, FTC, and state medical board advertising rules — the US equivalents of ASA, CAP Code, and MHRA. Clinical trust is not new territory for me.
Patient acquisition at scale
Every campaign at Red Castle Services was ultimately about getting patients to choose a provider. D2C healthcare conversion, at volume, with compliance built in from the start.
Player-coach operating model
I've defended strategy to boards and personally built funnels, run ad accounts, and A/B tested landing pages in the same week. Until the team is built — that's the job.
Mental health marketing experience
Psychologists and psychiatrists were among our client base at Red Castle. Running compliant acquisition campaigns for mental health practitioners is directly relevant to what ProblemShared needs.
